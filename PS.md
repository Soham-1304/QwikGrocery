# Problem Statement 85: QuickGrocery

Grocery shopping and delivery can become difficult when product discovery, cart management, delivery tracking and recurring orders are handled separately. QuickGrocery will provide a centralized Flutter interface for browsing groceries, managing a shopping cart, tracking deliveries and scheduling subscription orders.

## Objectives
- **UI/Widgets**: Build grocery dashboard, product catalog, product detail, cart, checkout, delivery tracking and subscription-order screens. Use SearchBar, Filters, GridView/ListView, Cards, Quantity controls, Forms, Date/Time Pickers, Map components, Buttons, Dialogs and status indicators.
- **Styling/Theming**: Apply Material 3 with clear product, cart, delivery and subscription states, accessible typography, consistent product cards, quantity controls, delivery progress indicators and responsive layouts.
- **Dart/Flutter Logic**: Create models for products, cart items, orders, deliveries and subscriptions; implement product filtering, quantity calculations, cart totals, delivery status updates, subscription scheduling, validation, state management and navigation.
- **Figma**: Design the complete flow from product catalog → select products → manage cart → checkout → track delivery → configure subscription order.

## Outcomes
- Develop a functional Flutter prototype demonstrating the core workflow of a grocery delivery service.
- Apply Dart and Flutter concepts to create structured, interactive, maintainable and responsive application screens.
- Translate the approved Figma user flow into a consistent Material 3 interface with appropriate widgets, calculations, scheduling and state handling.
- Demonstrate product browsing, cart management, delivery tracking and subscription orders through a complete start-to-finish user journey.

## Deliverables
- **Figma Design**: Grocery dashboard, product catalog/detail, cart, checkout, delivery tracking, subscription orders and navigation flow.
- **UI/Widgets**: Product cards, search/filter controls, quantity controls, cart list, checkout forms, delivery map/status, subscription forms, dialogs and navigation.
- **Styling/Theming**: Material 3 theme, product/cart/delivery indicators, typography, spacing, product imagery, progress states and responsive behavior.
- **Dart/Flutter Logic**: Product/cart/order/delivery/subscription models, filtering, quantity and total calculations, validation, scheduling, delivery states, state management and navigation.
- **Integration**: Integrate grocery catalog, inventory/order backend, delivery tracking/location services and subscription-order storage.
- **Responsive Prototype**: Complete working journey from browsing products and managing the cart through checkout, delivery tracking and subscription-order setup.
